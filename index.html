Your CarBrowser session

Websites run on your device, not as streamed screenshots. Typing, scrolling, sound and ordinary video use your browser directly. Loading still depends on the website, your connection and the relay.

The server relays public web traffic. It does not keep a browser profile or a CarBrowser account for you. Website cookies and cache are stored in this browser; use Clear browsing data to remove them. This is not a VPN or an anonymity service. Avoid sensitive accounts.

This browser-side proxy is experimental. Some sites, logins, CAPTCHAs and DRM-protected video may not work. Local networks, app services and non-web ports are blocked.
